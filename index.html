03 / About

One person, all in.

Poller Apps is a one-person workshop — a solo developer designing, coding, and polishing every detail, driven purely by love for apps and games.

The love of poker runs through it all: home games with friends became Sogrim, the free poker settler, and now Poker TD is on the way. This is the early road: small releases, built in the open, one at a time.

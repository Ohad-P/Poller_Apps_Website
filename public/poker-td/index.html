Poker TD

Build your hand. Defend the route.

Coming soon — first playable build on the way.

A poker tower defense game

Poker TD is an upcoming fantasy tower defense game where poker hands become towers. Raiders march down a winding lane toward your gates. Your only weapon is a deck of cards: every round you deal a poker hand, and whatever you make takes stone, wood, and crystal form on the battlefield to hold the line.

How it plays

  1. 01 · Deal Every round opens with five cards from a fresh 52-card deck.
  2. 02 · Replace once Keep your hand or swap up to five cards. One decision, then the hand is final.
  3. 03 · Raise a tower Your final hand becomes a tower — all ten poker hands, from High Card to Royal Flush, map to ten different towers with their own attack, range, and role.
  4. 04 · Defend the route Place the tower, and the next wave marches. Towers fire on their own while you watch the lane, spend battle gold on upgrades, and guard your 20 lives.
